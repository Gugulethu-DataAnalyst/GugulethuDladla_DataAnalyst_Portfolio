German Credit Risk Analysis & Default Prediction

A data-driven credit risk assessment framework built on the German Credit Dataset. Combines exploratory data analysis, multi-factor risk segmentation, and supervised machine learning classification to identify high-risk loan applicants and minimize financial default exposure.

View GitHub Repository

Business Problem & Goals

  • Replace inconsistent rule-based credit screening with an objective, data-driven probability-of-default model.
  • Identify primary socioeconomic and financial drivers of creditworthiness (savings reserves, housing stability, credit history, and loan exposure).
  • Address class imbalance to maximize recall on potential defaulters while maintaining sustainable loan approval rates.
  • Evaluate classification models using Precision, Recall, F1-Score, and ROC-AUC aligned with real-world lending risk appetite.

Key Findings & Outcomes

  • Identified an overall portfolio default rate of approximately 11.7%, heavily concentrated among borrowers with low savings, unstable housing, and high loan amounts.
  • Demonstrated compounding risk: borrowers experiencing multiple simultaneous adverse conditions exhibited substantially higher default probability than single-factor outliers.
  • Delivered a tuned classification model prioritizing high default capture (recall) to protect lenders against credit losses.
