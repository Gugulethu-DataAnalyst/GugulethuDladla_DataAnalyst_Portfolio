Self-Development Project • 2025

Discovery Vitality & Health Claims Analysis

End-to-end healthcare and wellness analytics study examining simulated Discovery Vitality member demographics, physical activity engagement (steps, gym visits, Vitality points), health risk factors (BMI, smoking status, chronic conditions), and medical insurance claims to uncover cost-driving patterns and optimize member engagement.

View GitHub Repository

Business Goals & Objectives

  • Phase 1 — Data Cleaning & Preparation: Merge and standardize Member, Claims, and Vitality datasets in Python (Pandas/NumPy), resolving survey null values and incorrect datatypes.
  • Phase 2 — Exploratory SQL Analysis: Extract and aggregate engagement levels, regional distribution, and claims behavior in MySQL.
  • Phase 3 — Dashboard Visualization: Build interactive stakeholder-facing Power BI dashboards covering demographics, claims efficiency, and physical activity.
  • Phase 4 — Insight Generation: Segment members by health risk profile to recommend targeted wellness incentives and reduce healthcare claim costs.

Project Outcomes

  • Delivered 5 interactive Power BI dashboards: Executive Overview, Member Demographics, Claims Analysis, Financial & Claims Efficiency, and Engagement & Activity.
  • Quantified correlations between active Vitality participation (steps, gym visits, check-ups) and lower high-cost chronic claim frequency.
  • Created regional and demographic segmentation to support personalized reward structures and proactive risk management.

SQL Showcase: Member Engagement vs. Claims Efficiency

MySQL analytical query joining member demographics, Vitality activity records, and medical claims to evaluate payout efficiency across risk tiers.

discovery_engagement_claims.sql 
SELECT 
    m.region,
    m.plan_type,
    CASE 
        WHEN v.vitality_points >= 30000 THEN 'High Engagement'
        WHEN v.vitality_points >= 15000 THEN 'Moderate Engagement'
        ELSE 'Low Engagement'
    END AS engagement_tier,
    COUNT(DISTINCT m.member_id) AS total_members,
    ROUND(AVG(v.monthly_gym_visits), 1) AS avg_gym_visits,
    ROUND(SUM(c.claim_amount), 2) AS total_claimed,
    ROUND(SUM(c.payout_amount) / NULLIF(SUM(c.claim_amount), 0) * 100, 2) AS payout_efficiency_pct
FROM members m
LEFT JOIN vitality_activity v ON m.member_id = v.member_id
LEFT JOIN medical_claims c ON m.member_id = c.member_id
GROUP BY m.region, m.plan_type, engagement_tier
ORDER BY m.region, total_claimed DESC;
